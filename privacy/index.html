Privacy policy: Terminal Launcher

Last updated: 2026-10-25. Terminal Launcher ("the app", package com.linkinxp.terminal) is a home-screen launcher for Android.

In short

The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. It does not send your data to the developer or to any server the developer runs. Your settings, layout and history stay on your phone.

What the app reads on your phone

These are used only on the device, to do what you see on screen, and are never uploaded:

  • Installed apps and their icons: to build the home screen and the app drawer.
  • Notification access (optional): if you switch it on, the app counts notifications per app to show badges, and reads which media is playing for the "now playing" panel. It never reads the text of your notifications.
  • Calendar (optional, runtime permission): to show upcoming events in the calendar panel.
  • Contacts (optional, runtime permission): only when you search in the app drawer, to show matching contact names.
  • Usage of the launcher: which apps you opened recently, to sort the drawer. Stored on the phone only.

What the app sends over the network

  • Weather (optional): if you turn the weather panel on, the app sends the latitude and longitude of the city you chose (not your device location) to the Open-Meteo weather service (open-meteo.com) to get a forecast, and the city name you typed to its geocoding service. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to those requests.
  • Web search: the search bar and the "search the web" row open your web browser with your query; the app itself does not send it anywhere.

Nothing else leaves the device.

Backups

"Export" writes a file to a place you choose; it contains your layout and settings. The app does not upload it.

Android's own backup (if you have it switched on in your phone settings) may also copy the app's layout and settings to your Google account, so they come back on a new phone or after a reinstall. That is handled by Android and your Google account, not by the app; it never includes your notifications, contacts or calendar.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes the new version will be published here. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/linkinxp/Terminal-Launcher/issues.
