Terminal is a home screen for people who like their phone to feel like a terminal.

It is built around a prompt: type to find and open an app, run commands to change the look, or ignore the prompt and just tap. Everything has a command and a settings screen.

LOOK
- 15 built-in color themes (matrix, nord, tokyo-night, gruvbox, dracula, catppuccin and more) and your own: theme add <name> <colors>
- A themed icon grid: every app's icon is redrawn in your theme colors (it uses the app's themed icon where there is one)
- Eight generated SVG wallpapers that follow your theme: circuit board, hex network, radar, tunnel, glitch skull, terminal windows, DNA helix, topographic map
- Falling-code wallpaper, ASCII-art wallpapers, or your system wallpaper
- CRT effects: scanlines, a sweeping scan band, glitch bars, flicker. All optional, all off with reduced motion
- Seven clock styles: pixel, 7-segment, FIGlet, binary, hex, unix epoch, plain; 12 or 24 hour

LAYOUT
- Icon grid or text list, four densities (6, 5, 4 or 3 columns) that also scale widgets
- Hold an icon and drag it anywhere: empty slots, other icons, other pages, the dock
- Panels you can place on any page: search, system stats, calendar (30 days, scrolls), weather, now playing, toggles (flashlight and shortcuts to wifi, bluetooth, sound, location)
- Android widgets, folders, a dock, quick tiles
- A one-line weather summary under the date

USEFUL
- App drawer with letter index, search, recent apps, and a search bar at the bottom within thumb reach
- Drawer search also finds your contacts, Android settings pages and the web
- Long-press an app for its own shortcuts
- Notification badges on icons (counts only, optional)
- Swipe down on the left half for notifications, on the right half for quick settings
- Import your layout from another launcher; back up and restore your whole setup to a file

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no analytics. Everything stays on your phone. The only network request is the optional weather panel, which sends the coordinates of the city you chose to Open-Meteo. Notification access (for badges and the now-playing panel) and contacts or calendar access are optional and used only on the device. Full policy: https://linkinxp.github.io/Terminal-Launcher/privacy/

Terminal replaces your home screen; you can switch back any time in Android settings.
